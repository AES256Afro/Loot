# M04B Dialogue Changes the Encounter

## Player promise

Talking is an encounter action, not a decorative pause before the inevitable fight. A returning actor remembers facts the player can inspect, social options show why they are available, and a committed choice can change initiative, enemy strength, allies, rewards, or whether combat happens at all.

This proof remains friendly to grinding. Refusing dialogue, failing a check, or choosing violence cannot delete gear, reduce the baseline reward, block the six-room route, or remove the Archive. There is no response timer.

## Proof actor

The first recurring actor is **Scrip, Repossessed Auditor**, promoted from the Form Auditor encounter. Scrip visibly escapes through a complaint hatch after the first office defeat. The save records a bounded typed summary:

- expedition seed and run index;
- Promoted Office room role;
- finishing party member and action;
- finishing damage type;
- whether pressure, Taunt, a critical hit, or a named equipment law participated;
- initial grievance, fear, respect, debt, and greed;
- one zone-relative growth choice.

Scrip returns only in a later expedition and supplements the Promoted Office. The actor cannot replace the critical route or repeatedly interrupt unrelated rooms.

## Social checks

The proof party has three visible social attributes:

- **Presence:** credible force, command, and willingness to accept attention;
- **Insight:** accurate reading of motives, contradictions, fear, and remembered facts;
- **Guile:** bargaining, leverage, misdirection, and profitable framing.

Every checked option shows attribute, relationship contribution, total leverage, difficulty, predicted result, and mechanical consequence before commitment. M04B uses deterministic threshold checks so reloading cannot fish for a better roll. Stats open or improve authored possibilities; they cannot overwrite an actor's core motive.

## Return encounter branches

Scrip's return begins in a warning posture before combat:

1. **Cite the Record:** Insight plus Scrip's fear can prove that another fight is professionally unsound. Success ends the encounter, clears the room, and grants the normal reward.
2. **Offer Partnership:** Guile plus Scrip's greed and respect can exploit a shared threat from the replacement office. Success removes Scrip from the enemy side, damages the replacement staff, and grants opening Guard before combat.
3. **Taunt the Audit:** Presence plus grievance forces a fight on favorable initiative. Scrip returns slightly stronger but begins Weakened and Exposed.
4. **End Parley:** starts the legal encounter without a check or hidden penalty.

Every branch preserves the route and baseline deterministic reward.

## The Bent Pipe

After the return encounter, the Hearthfold can route the party to **The Bent Pipe**, a semi-safe bar represented by its own warm first-person set. Weapons are checked. Insults are not. Scrip appears there according to the current relationship posture.

An actor who helped during the shared-danger branch can become a friend only after a second explicit choice at the bar. This is not one universal charisma roll. It requires remembered cooperation, current self-preservation, compatible greed, and a follow-up conversation. Other branches can produce paid neutrality, information, mutual respect, or continued rivalry.

## Acceptance evidence

M04B is complete when:

- a first Form Auditor defeat creates and saves the typed Scrip record;
- a later Promoted Office presents inspectable social choices before combat;
- one choice prevents combat, one changes combat, and one intensifies combat;
- the baseline reward and critical route survive every outcome;
- Scrip accurately references at least two stored facts;
- The Bent Pipe is visually distinct from the dungeon and can be visited without a timer;
- the shared-danger route can move Scrip from enemy to temporary ally to friend;
- save/load retains memory, relationship, appearances, bar history, and unresolved promises;
- automated tests, a native macOS route, evidence captures, exports, and remote `main` publication pass.
